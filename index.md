I am a recent computer science graduate from Howard University (B.S., minor in
mathematics) interested in machine learning, algorithms, and the mathematical
foundations of learning.

This site is where I work things out in the open. I implement algorithms from
first principles — neural networks with only NumPy, regression from its normal
equations, classic graph and data-structure algorithms — and write down the
derivations that make them work. The goal is understanding: why an algorithm is
correct, what it costs, and where its assumptions break.
